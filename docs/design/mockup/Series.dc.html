What Structures adds to the series

Challenge ladder

  1. River crossing: 20 t, under $30k
  2. Overhang: hold 5 t out over the water
  3. Heavy haul: two 25 t loads, piers allowed
  4. Snap test: survive losing any one member
  5. Lightest bridge duel (vs a friend or the optimizer)
  6. Material roulette
  7. Shake table: find the resonance
  8. 100 floors, under budget
  9. Tame the sway: tune the mass damper
  10. The lean of Pisa, and the fix
  11. Liquefaction: keep it upright
  12. Boss: quake + hurricane on soft clay
